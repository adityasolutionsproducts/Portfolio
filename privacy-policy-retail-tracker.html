Retail Tracker collects only the information needed to operate your shop. Data is entered by you or your staff, or generated by the device with your permission.

02

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App's core features: POS sales, stock, purchases, dues, expenses, staff, and multi-branch management.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your business data private to your account.
  • To keep your branches synced in real time across devices via Firestore.
  • To let you back up and restore your own data, and to export invoices, reports, and CSV files.
  • To send a customer's or supplier's own bill or dues reminder to their saved WhatsApp number, only when you tap the WhatsApp share button — this action is always user-initiated.
  • To show local notifications for app events and reminders.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your data to third parties.
03

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and business data are stored in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. A copy of your working data is also cached locally in your browser's local storage to support offline use, and is synced back to Firestore when you're online.

04

05

WhatsApp Sharing

The App can open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message (a bill, invoice, or dues reminder) addressed to a customer's or supplier's saved number when you tap the WhatsApp button. This uses a wa.me link and WhatsApp's own sharing mechanism on your device. The App does not automate or send messages without you tapping the button, and does not access your WhatsApp account or message history.

06

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore — authentication and data storage. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp — only invoked when you choose to share a bill, invoice, or reminder; governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy for that action.
07

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data is shared only:

  • With Google's infrastructure providers (Firebase) strictly to operate the App.
  • When you personally choose to share a bill, report, or export via WhatsApp or your device's share options.
08

Data Retention & Deletion

Your business data is retained in Firestore for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual records at any time within the App. To request full account and data deletion, contact us using the details below — we will delete your account data from our systems within a reasonable time, except where retention is required by law.

09

Data Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication and Firestore's built-in security rules to restrict access to your data to your own authenticated account. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10

Children's Privacy

Retail Tracker is a business tool intended for use by adults managing shop operations. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can manage most of this directly within the App, or contact us for assistance.

12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The "Effective date" above will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact Aditya Solutions — we're happy to help with access, correction, export, or full account & data deletion requests.
