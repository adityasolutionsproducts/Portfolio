Puja Sarathi collects only the information needed to run your committee. Data is entered by you or the members you invite, or generated by the device with your permission.

We do not collect: advertising identifiers, browsing history outside the App, your location, or your phone's personal contacts. We do not sell or rent your committee's or members' data to any third party.

02

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App's core features: members, chanda/collection tracking, and event management for your committee.
  • To authenticate your account and keep each committee's data visible only to its invited members.
  • To manage roles and invite codes so the right people get the right level of access.
  • To let you export member and chanda lists to CSV/Excel, and share summaries.
  • To show local notifications for app events and reminders.
  • To keep your working data available offline on your device and synced when you're back online.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your data to third parties.
03

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and committee data are stored in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. A copy of your working data is also cached locally in your browser's local storage to support offline use, and is synced back to Firestore when you're online.

04

05

WhatsApp Sharing

The App can open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message (such as a chanda reminder or event notice) when you tap the WhatsApp button. This uses a wa.me link and WhatsApp's own sharing mechanism on your device. The App does not automate or send messages without you tapping the button, and does not access your WhatsApp account or message history.

06

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore — authentication and data storage. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp — only invoked when you choose to share a message; governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy for that action.
07

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data is shared only:

  • With Google's infrastructure providers (Firebase) strictly to operate the App.
  • Within your own committee — members you invite can see the committee's shared data, according to their role.
  • When you personally choose to share a message or export via WhatsApp or your device's share options.
08

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained in Firestore for as long as your account is active. Members and records can be deleted within the App by users with the appropriate role. To request full account and data deletion, contact us using the details below — we will delete your account data from our systems within a reasonable time, except where retention is required by law.

09

Data Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication and Firestore's built-in security rules to restrict access to a committee's data to its invited, authenticated members. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10

Children's Privacy

Puja Sarathi is a committee-management tool intended for use by adults organising community events. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can manage most of this directly within the App, or contact us for assistance.

12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The "Effective date" above will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact Aditya Solutions — we're happy to help with access, correction, export, or full account & data deletion requests.
