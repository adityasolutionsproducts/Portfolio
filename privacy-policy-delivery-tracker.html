Privacy Policy

Delivery Tracker

Effective date: August 25, 2026  •  App version 3.2.0  •  Developed by Aditya Solutions

This Privacy Policy explains how Delivery Tracker ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the App. Delivery Tracker is a delivery and parcel management tool built for businesses to manage customers, addresses, and parcel scans. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Delivery Tracker collects only the information needed to operate its delivery-management features. Data is entered by you (the business user) or generated by the device with your permission.

CategoryWhat's collectedWhy
Account information Email address and password (via Firebase Authentication) To sign in and secure your account
Customer & business data Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, company records, parcel/delivery entries you create Core app functionality — managing your customers and deliveries
Location data Precise GPS coordinates, only when you tap "Get My Location" To auto-fill a customer's delivery address
Camera data Live camera feed for barcode/QR scanning (processed on-device via Google ML Kit) Scanning parcels; no photos or video are captured, stored, or uploaded
Backup files A JSON export of your app data, created only when you choose "Backup" Letting you save or share a copy of your data via your device's Share sheet
We do not collect: advertising identifiers, browsing history outside the App, contacts from your phone (unless you explicitly export data to Google Contacts), or any data you have not entered into the App yourself.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App's core features: customer records, delivery tracking, parcel scanning, and payment recording.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your business data private to your account.
  • To auto-fill addresses using reverse-geocoding of GPS coordinates you choose to capture.
  • To let you back up and restore your own data, and to export customer records to Google Contacts if you choose to.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and business data are stored in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, Google's cloud infrastructure, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. Some data is also cached locally on your device (via IndexedDB / local storage) to support offline use, and is synced back to Firestore when you're online.

4. Permissions the App Requests

All sensitive permissions are requested only at the moment they're needed — never at launch — and you can decline them (some features will simply be unavailable).

PermissionRequested when
CameraYou open the parcel barcode/QR scanner
Precise/Approximate locationYou tap "Get My Location" on the address screen
Storage (Android 9 and below only)You create or restore a backup file
Internet / network stateAlways — required to sync with Firebase

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore — authentication and data storage. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • Google ML Kit (Barcode Scanning) — processes the camera feed on-device to read barcodes/QR codes; image data is not uploaded or stored.
  • Google Contacts — only if you explicitly choose to export customer records as a CSV file to import into Google Contacts yourself; the App does not access your existing contacts.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data is shared only:

  • With Google's infrastructure providers (Firebase) strictly to operate the App.
  • When you personally choose to share a backup file or exported CSV via your device's Share sheet (e.g., saving to Drive, sending via WhatsApp, or importing to Google Contacts).

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained in Firestore for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual customer or parcel records at any time within the App. To request full account and data deletion, contact us using the details below — we will delete your account data from our systems within a reasonable time, except where retention is required by law.

8. Data Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication and Firestore's built-in security rules to restrict access to your data to your own authenticated account. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Children's Privacy

Delivery Tracker is a business tool intended for use by adults managing delivery operations. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. You can manage most of this directly within the App, or contact us for assistance.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The "Effective date" above will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
