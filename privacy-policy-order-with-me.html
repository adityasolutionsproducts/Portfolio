01

Information We Collect

Order With Me collects the information needed to run your business operations through the App. Data is entered by you or your staff, or generated by the device with your permission.

We do not collect: advertising identifiers, browsing history outside the App, or your phone's personal contacts. We do not sell or rent your business or customer data to any third party.

02

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App's core ERP features: orders, deliveries, stock, billing, and fleet management.
  • To authenticate your account and enforce role-based access within your business.
  • To generate and save PDF/Excel documents (bills, quotations, purchase orders, stickers, reports) directly to your device.
  • To send a shop's own invoice to that shop's saved WhatsApp number, only when you tap the WhatsApp share button — this action is always user-initiated.
  • To show Android system notifications mirroring in-app alerts.
  • To check for required app updates via a minimum-version check (see Section 5).
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
03

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your business data is stored in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. Generated PDFs, stickers, and reports are saved locally to your device's public Download/Order With Me/ folder, organized into Bill, Stickers, Barcodes, and Report subfolders.

04

Permissions the App Requests

All sensitive permissions are requested only at the moment they're needed — never at launch.

PermissionRequested when
CameraYou open the barcode/QR scanner or upload a photo
Notifications (Android 13+)The first time the App sends you a notification
Storage (Android 9 and below only)The first time you export a bill, sticker, or report
Internet / network stateAlways — required to sync with Firebase

On Android 10 and above, no storage permission is required at all — files are saved through the system's scoped Downloads access.

05

WhatsApp Sharing

The App can send a generated bill PDF directly to a shop's saved WhatsApp number when you tap the WhatsApp button. This uses WhatsApp's own sharing mechanism on your device (or falls back to the system share sheet, or a wa.me link if WhatsApp isn't installed). The App does not automate or send messages without you tapping this button, and does not access your WhatsApp account or message history.

06

App Version Check

On launch and when resuming, the App checks a publicly readable Firestore document to see if a minimum app version is required. This check only reads a version number and update message — no personal data is sent as part of this check. If your version is too old, you'll be prompted to update.

07

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore — authentication and data storage. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp — only invoked when you choose to share a bill; governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy for that action.
08

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data is shared only:

  • With Google's infrastructure providers (Firebase) strictly to operate the App.
  • When you personally choose to share a bill via WhatsApp or the system share sheet.
09

Data Retention & Deletion

Your business data is retained in Firestore for as long as your account/business is active. Records can be deleted within the App by users with the appropriate role. To request full account and data deletion, contact us using the details below.

10

Data Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication and Firestore's built-in security rules to restrict data access to authenticated users of your business. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

11

Children's Privacy

Order With Me ERP is a business tool intended for use by adults managing shop/order operations. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Most of this can be managed directly within the App by an Admin/Super Admin role, or by contacting us for assistance.

13

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The "Effective date" above will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact Aditya Solutions — we're happy to help with access, correction, export, or full account & data deletion requests.
